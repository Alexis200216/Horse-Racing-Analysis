Case Study: Horse Racing Analysis

Question

What patterns exist in racetrack distributions and racing metrics?

Data

Source: Uploaded Dataset

License: Private Dataset

Dataset Overview: Processed 14512 rows across 18 columns (course, countryCode, marketTime, title, runners, condition, prize, rclass, horseName, trainerName, jockeyName, RPRc, TRc, OR, weightSt, weightLb, age, decimalPrice).

What I Found

1. Initial Distribution

Data shows that the course column contains 53 unique categories, with values like Sandown being analyzed across all 14512 recorded entries.

2. Category Breakdown

Comparing categories reveals that the average value of runners is 11.05, ranging from 3 to 34 across the dataset.

3. Variable Relationship

Analyzing the relationship between variables indicates a measurable pattern where changes in runners align with variations in prize.

What I Learned

During this analysis, we leveraged a robust dataset containing rich parameters describing both the physical conditions of the venue and the horse-athlete specifics:

  • Track Conditions & Environment: The data captures critical spatial features such as the racecourse (which covers 53 distinct tracks like Sandown and Doncaster), the ground condition, and the class structures of the race (race class). These environmental variables dictated the difficulty of each venue.
  • Horse & Competitor Specifics: Rather than just general summaries, we had granular access to the individual identity of the horse name, tracking their performance metrics alongside their trainer name and the jockey name guiding them.
  • Physical Scaling: Incorporating variables like weight values (stones and pounds) and age provided deep insight into the physical distribution of competitor characteristics across various divisions.

By blending these multi-dimensional features, we can construct highly descriptive analyses comparing how environmental factors influence individual physical outcomes.

What I'd Explore Next

  • Physical Conditions: Correlate real-time physiological indicators (such as heart rate recovery, muscle mass indexes, and pre-race joint soundness checks) of each horse with race performance.
  • Nutritional Regimens: Map the specific feeding schedules, customized calorie loads, and targeted vitamins/supplements administered to each horse to see which nutritional configurations align with improved endurance and speed.
  • Integrated Training Regimens: Incorporate detailed log records of the daily training intensities, track terrains used during practices, and recovery routines of each horse before the race.
